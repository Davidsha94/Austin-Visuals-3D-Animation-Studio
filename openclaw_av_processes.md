- *Purpose:* Qualify inbound opportunities quickly, collect enough information to estimate scope, and decide whether Austin Visuals should quote the job, decline it, or refer it out.

### Relationship Calling Program  
**Sources:**  
- `Sales Relationship Caller - Training Notes - From Training Video - Training Instructions.docx` fileciteturn5file0

- *Purpose:* Reconnect with older leads and past clients to build trust, uncover future needs, collect testimonials, and generate sales without hard selling.
- *Owner / Roles:* Relationship caller / relationship marketing director / relationship sales or marketing role; Matt as owner/manager.
- *Inputs:* Assigned lead list, past contact date, project background, call script, spreadsheet.
- *Outputs / Deliverables:* Call notes, testimonials, business/personal needs, future-opportunity flags, sales escalations.
- *Trigger / When to run:* Leads older than 45 days since last contact; ongoing maintenance of past relationships. fileciteturn5file0
- *Step-by-Step:*  
  1. Start with assigned leads and call until you reach the person.  
  2. Prioritize higher-value historical leads.  
  3. Use the script conversationally; avoid hard-pressure selling.  
  4. Ask why they did or did not buy, what they value, and whether they have future visual needs.  
  5. If they were a past client, ask about results, ROI, and whether they will provide a testimonial.  
  6. Log everything in the spreadsheet and email weekly observations to Matt.  
  7. Mark do-not-call contacts and stop outreach if requested.
- *Key Tools / Links:* Call spreadsheet, phone, email follow-up template.
- *SLAs / Timelines:* Calls average 5–20 minutes; weekly feedback expected; weekly log email expected. fileciteturn5file0
- *Risk / Escalation Notes:* Assistants are not considered reliable decision makers; do-not-call leads must be visibly marked; if asked for pricing, callers should collect specs and escalate to sales/owner rather than quote directly. fileciteturn5file0
